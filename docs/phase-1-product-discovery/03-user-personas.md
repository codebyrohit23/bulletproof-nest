## Owner

### Description

The Owner is the business owner or agency founder.

This is the highest authority within an organization.

The Owner is responsible for business growth, team management, and overall platform configuration.

---

### Responsibilities

- Manage organization
- Invite users
- Manage subscription
- Configure pipelines
- View reports
- Manage integrations
- Configure settings

---

### Goals

- Increase lead conversion
- Track sales performance
- Ensure every lead is followed up
- Monitor team productivity

---

### Pain Points

- Leads get lost
- No visibility into team performance
- Sales agents forget follow-ups
- Multiple communication channels

---

### Permissions

Full access

---

### Typical Workflow

Login

↓

Dashboard

↓

View Lead Statistics

↓

Assign Leads

↓

Review Team Performance

↓

Check Reports

↓

Logout

## Sales Manager

### Description

Manages the sales team and monitors lead progress.

Responsible for assigning leads and ensuring follow-ups.

### Responsibilities

- Assign leads
- Monitor pipeline
- Review agent performance
- Create tasks
- Review timelines

### Goals

- Faster lead response
- Better pipeline management
- No forgotten follow-ups

### Pain Points

- Manual lead assignment
- No centralized tracking

### Permissions

Cannot manage subscription.

Cannot delete organization.

Can manage assigned teams.

### Workflow

Login

↓

Dashboard

↓

Assign Lead

↓

Monitor Pipeline

↓

Review Activities

## Sales Agent

### Description

Handles customer communication and converts leads into customers.

### Responsibilities

- Call customers
- Reply on WhatsApp
- Add notes
- Update lead stage
- Complete tasks

### Goals

- Close more deals
- Stay organized

### Pain Points

- Too many follow-ups
- Scattered conversations

### Permissions

Can only access assigned leads.

Cannot manage organization.

Cannot access billing.

### Workflow

Login

↓

Assigned Leads

↓

Call Customer

↓

Add Note

↓

Move Pipeline

↓

Complete Task
