# Business Capabilities

## Purpose

This document defines the core business capabilities provided by LeadFlow AI.

A business capability is something the platform enables a business to do.

Capabilities describe business value, not technical implementation.

Multiple modules may collaborate to deliver a single capability.

---

# What is a Business Capability?

A business capability answers one question.

"What valuable business outcome does the platform provide?"

Examples

- Capture Leads
- Assign Leads
- Track Follow-ups
- Convert Leads

Not

- Repository
- Controller
- API
- DTO
